Read this before acting

  • Critical rows are individual work. Privileged accounts that cannot satisfy MFA after retirement. Register a FIDO2 key or platform passkey and verify with a real test sign-in. Check emergency-access accounts specifically; they are the ones most likely to have a phone number attached that nobody has looked at in a year.
  • Register the new method before removing the phone method. Doing it in the other order creates the lockout you are working to prevent.
  • A large Moderate count means legacy per-user MFA. Those users have a phone method registered but do not resolve into modern AMP scope. They are still in scope for the retirement. This assessment cannot read legacy per-user MFA service settings; validate that population manually.
  • Policy scope is not effective sign-in behaviour. Conditional Access is not evaluated here. A user in AMP scope may never be challenged, and a user outside it may still be blocked by a grant control this assessment does not read.
  • Guests follow a separate Microsoft timeline for passkey support. Validate B2B readiness independently before assuming a guest can register on the same schedule as a member.

Scope and method

What was assessed

  • Every enabled user object returned by Graph, members and guests, with full pagination.
  • SMS and voice authentication methods policy include and exclude targets, resolved to users through nested group membership.
  • The authentication methods registration report, per user.
  • Registration campaign state, which Microsoft sets to Microsoft managed for in-scope tenants on 1 September 2026.

What was not

  • Disabled users. The registration report does not return them.
  • Legacy per-user MFA service settings. Reading them requires beta endpoints and broader permissions than this assessment holds.
  • Conditional Access. Policy scope is not the same as being challenged at sign-in.
  • Non-human accounts. Shared mailboxes and service accounts appear as ordinary users and should be reviewed before they become tickets.
